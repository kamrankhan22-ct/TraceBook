Delete your TraceBook account

Last updated: 11 October 2026

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open TraceBook and sign in.
  2. Tap My space, then the About tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm. If you use Google, you will be asked to sign in once more. If you use a password, enter it.

What is deleted: your profile and photo, your messages in chats you can access, your blocks, the feedback you sent, your study decks and flashcards, your group memberships, the groups you own (with everything inside), and your sign-in account.

What may remain: reactions you left, without your name, and reports about or from you, which are kept for safety. Short-term backups and provider logs may keep data for a little while.

If you cannot open the app

Email tracebooksix12@gmail.com from the address you used to sign up, with the subject "Delete my TraceBook account". We will confirm your request and delete your data, and we aim to finish within 30 days.

Just want to remove some things?

You can delete your own messages (tap the message time, then Delete), delete private chats, leave or delete groups, and clear your profile details in My space.
