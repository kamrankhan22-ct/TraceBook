Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

TraceBook ("we", "us") is a chat app that looks like a ruled notebook. This page explains what information TraceBook handles, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

1. Information we handle

Account. Your email address and name. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your Google profile photo link. If you sign in with email and password, your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see or store it.

Profile. What you choose to add: a photo (resized small), status, mood, pronouns, city, interests, a link, your colour, the date you joined, and when you were last online (unless you hide your online status).

Content. Messages, replies, reactions, the traces and groups you create or join, invite codes, private chats, people you block, reports you file, feedback you send (rating, text, your name and email), and the study decks and flashcards you create in Recall, your Brain Test results, and the tasks, notes and updates you add in a group's Team workspace.

Technical data. Our service providers may keep standard technical logs such as IP address, device and browser type.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, microphone or camera.
  • We only read a photo if you choose one in My space.
  • We do not read your private chats for advertising or profiling.

3. Why we use your information

  • To run TraceBook: sign you in, show your profile, deliver messages, groups and notifications.
  • To keep people safe: handle blocks and reports, and remove content or accounts that break our Terms.
  • To improve the app and answer your feedback.

4. Who can see what

WhereWho can see it
Public tracesEveryone who is signed in to TraceBook
Group traces and Team workspace (tasks, notes, updates)Only the members of that group
Private chatsOnly the two people in the chat
Your profileEveryone who is signed in (your email is not shown)
Online statusEveryone who is signed in, unless you hide it in My space, Privacy
Study decks, flashcards and Brain Test resultsOnly you
Reports and feedbackOnly the TraceBook operator

The person who operates TraceBook can access stored data through the Firebase console. This includes private chats and group messages. We use this only to run the service, keep people safe, and give support.

5. Service providers and transfers

TraceBook uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore database) to sign you in and store data, and Vercel to host the website. They process data for us under their own terms and privacy policies. Data may be stored and processed in countries other than yours.

6. Sharing because of the law or safety

We may share information if the law, a court or a government authority requires it, or if we believe it is needed to protect someone's safety or to stop serious harm or illegal activity. Where the law allows, we will try to tell you first.

7. What is stored on your device

TraceBook keeps these in your browser's local storage: your theme, message text size, Enter key choice, and the time you last sent feedback. Your browser also keeps your sign-in session. If you turn on notifications, your browser asks for permission and shows them. You can clear this data in your browser settings.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your information until you delete it or delete your account. You can delete your own messages, chats you are part of, and groups you own. You can delete your whole account in the app: My space, About, Delete my account. This removes your profile, your sign-in, your messages in chats you can access, your blocks, your feedback, your study decks, flashcards and Brain Test results, the tasks and notes you added to groups you do not own, your group memberships and the groups you own. Reactions you left may remain without your name, and reports about or from you may be kept for safety. Backups and provider logs may keep data for a short time.

If you cannot use the app, see how to ask for deletion.

9. Your rights and choices

  • See and copy: ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Correct: edit your profile in My space, or ask us to fix anything else.
  • Delete: delete messages, chats, groups or your whole account.
  • Control: hide your online status, block people, and report content in the app.
  • Complain: if you are unhappy with how we handled your information, tell us at the email below. We aim to reply within 30 days. You may also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country.

10. Children

TraceBook is not for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will remove it. Laws in your country may set a higher age for using online services, and you are responsible for following them.

11. Security and data breaches

We use Firebase security rules so that people only reach data they are allowed to see. No online service is perfectly secure, so please do not share secrets or sensitive personal information in chats. If a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If the changes are important, we will also tell you in the app. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.
